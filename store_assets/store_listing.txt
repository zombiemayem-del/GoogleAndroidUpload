APP NAME: Roll & Dash: 2048 Race

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 chars):
Roll, merge matching numbers and race to 2048! A fun and colorful number game.

FULL DESCRIPTION:
Roll & Dash: 2048 Race is a colorful number-merging game where you guide a ball down a track and combine matching numbers to grow bigger.

HOW TO PLAY:
- Swipe left and right to steer your ball
- Collect balls with the same number to merge and double your value
- Dodge thorns that cut your number in half
- Balance carefully on narrow bridges
- Reach the finish ramp and climb for bonus score multipliers

FEATURES:
- Easy one-finger swipe controls suitable for all ages
- Colorful 3D graphics with fun particle effects
- Numbers go from 2 all the way up to 2048
- Finish ramp with increasing score multipliers
- Quick sessions perfect for playing on the go

CATEGORY: Casual
CONTENT RATING: Everyone
